Who is Brr Brr Patapim?

Brr Brr Patapim is a forest creature that’s part monkey and part tree, with a big nose and huge feet. In its original poem, the narrator says its hat is full of Slim: “il mio cappello è pieno di Slim”.

The “brr brr” is said with a rolled R, like a shiver, not spelled out letter by letter.
