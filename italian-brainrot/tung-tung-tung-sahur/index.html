Who is Tung Tung Tung Sahur?

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is a wooden log with a face, holding a baseball bat. The story goes that it knocks three times, “tung tung tung”, and comes for anyone who doesn’t wake up for sahur.

It’s one of the biggest Italian brainrot characters even though it isn’t Italian at all: it comes from Indonesia. During Ramadan, people walk through neighborhoods banging drums and wooden instruments to wake everyone for sahur, the meal before dawn. The “tung tung” is the sound of that knocking.
