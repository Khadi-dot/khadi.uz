What does “no cap” mean?

No lie. "Cap" means a lie, and 🧢 means someone is lying.

What it means

No cap means “no lie” or “for real”. People add it to show they’re being serious. “Cap” on its own means a lie, so “that’s cap” means “that’s not true”, and the 🧢 emoji is used to call out someone lying.

Where it comes from

Cap meaning a lie comes from African American English and spread through hip-hop in the 2010s before taking over social media. “No cap” and “fr” (for real) are now some of the most common things kids add to the end of a sentence.
