What does “bussin” mean?

Extremely good. Mostly used for food.

What it means

Bussin means extremely good, and it’s mostly used for food. Something that’s “bussin bussin” is even better. It can describe other things too, like a song or an outfit, but a really good plate of plov is the classic use.

Where it comes from

Bussin comes from African American English, where it has meant “really good” for years. It went mainstream on TikTok around 2020 and 2021, especially in food reviews.

How to use it

  • “this plov is bussin.”
  • “grandma’s somsa? bussin bussin.”
  • “the new song is lowkey bussin.”
