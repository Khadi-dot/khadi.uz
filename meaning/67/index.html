What does “6 7 (67)” mean?

Means nothing at all. Say it with both palms up, moving them up and down. Got so big that Dictionary.com picked it as its Word of the Year for 2025.

What it means

Six seven (written 67 or 6-7) means nothing, and that is exactly the point. Kids shout it whenever the numbers 6 and 7 come up, or for no reason at all, usually with both palms up, moving them up and down like a scale. The 🤲 emoji stands in for the hand motion.

Teachers report entire classes yelling it when a page number or an answer is 67. Trying to make it mean something is a sure sign you’re an unc.

Where it comes from

It comes from “Doot Doot (6 7)”, a 2024 song by Philadelphia rapper Skrilla. The line got paired with basketball edits of LaMelo Ball, who is 6 feet 7 inches tall, and in 2025 a clip of a kid shouting “six seven!” at a basketball game spread the hand motion everywhere. Dictionary.com picked 6-7 as its Word of the Year for 2025.

How to use it

  • “okay class, turn to page 67.” (the whole class loses it)
  • “how tall is he?” “6 7.”
