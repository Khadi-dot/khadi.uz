What does “aura farming” mean?

Doing cool things on purpose just to gain aura. Made famous in 2025 by a kid dancing on the front of a racing boat in Indonesia.

What it means

Aura farming means doing something cool on purpose, slowly and confidently, so everyone notices: a slow-motion walk, a calm stare at the camera, a dramatic pose at exactly the right moment. It can be a compliment (“he’s aura farming and it’s working”) or a roast (“stop aura farming, we can see you trying”).

Where it comes from

The term blew up in July 2025 thanks to Rayyan Arkan Dikha, an 11-year-old from Indonesia who danced calmly at the front of a racing boat during Pacu Jalur, a traditional boat race in Riau. Clips of his effortless moves went viral around the world, people called it the perfect example of aura farming, and sports teams and celebrities posted their own versions of the dance.
