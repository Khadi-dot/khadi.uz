What does “goated” mean?

The greatest. From G.O.A.T., greatest of all time.

What it means

Goated means the greatest, legendary, untouchable. A goated player is the best at what they do, a goated snack is the best snack, and a goated teacher is the one everyone wants. “Goated with the sauce” is the extra-strong version.

Where it comes from

It comes from GOAT, short for “greatest of all time”, which sports fans have used for decades. Turning it into an adjective became popular online around 2020.
