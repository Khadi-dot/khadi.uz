What does “sheesh” mean?

Said when something is impressive. The more e’s, the more impressive.

What it means

Sheesh is what you say when something is impressive. The more e’s, the more impressed you are: sheeeeeesh. It’s usually stretched out slowly, sometimes with a squinting face.

Where it comes from

Sheesh has been around as an exclamation for a long time, but it went viral on TikTok in 2021, where people said it, drawn out, after anything impressive.

How to use it

  • “sheeeeesh, you got 100%?”
  • “he scored from the halfway line. sheesh.”
  • “new shoes? sheesh.”
