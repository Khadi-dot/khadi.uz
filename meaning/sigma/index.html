What does “sigma” mean?

A lone wolf who is cool without trying. Allegedly at the top of the social ladder.

What it means

A sigma is cool, independent and doesn’t care what anyone thinks. They don’t follow the crowd, don’t need approval and rarely text back. Kids say “sigma” about people, choices and even food (“sigma breakfast”).

Most of the time it’s a joke. Calling someone “so sigma” for doing something completely normal is half the fun.

Where it comes from

It comes from the “sigma male”, an online idea that sorted men into alpha, beta and so on, with sigma as the lone wolf who doesn’t play the game. Memes and dramatic movie edits made the word huge around 2021 and 2022, and it slowly turned into an ironic compliment. The 🗿 moai emoji is its unofficial mascot.

How to use it

  • “didn’t text back. sigma behavior.”
  • “he ate lunch alone with headphones on. sigma.”
  • “sigma rule #1: never give up the aux cord.”
