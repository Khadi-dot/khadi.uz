What does “ohio” mean?

Anything weird, cursed or unexplainable. Nothing personal against the actual state.

What it means

When something is strange, cursed or makes no sense, it’s “so Ohio”. A cow on the metro, a cat driving a car, a teacher who gives no homework: only in Ohio. It’s also a mild insult: “your outfit is Ohio” means it’s weird, and not in a good way.

It isn’t really about the US state. Ohio just became the internet’s official home of anything bizarre.

Where it comes from

The “Only in Ohio” meme took off on TikTok and YouTube around 2022, with edits showing Ohio as a place where impossible things happen every day. The joke spread so far that kids who have never been anywhere near the United States use it.

How to use it

  • “a goose chased my bus today. only in Ohio.”
  • “why is your sandwich blue? that’s so Ohio.”
  • “this level is Ohio difficulty.”
