What does “mogging” mean?

Looking so much better than someone that they disappear next to you.

What it means

To mog someone is to outshine them just by standing next to them, usually by being better looking, taller or more confident. If you get mogged, you look average in comparison. Things can mog each other too, like a new phone mogging the old one.

Where it comes from

Mog comes from AMOG, short for “alpha male of the group”, an old pickup-artist term. It spread through looksmaxxing communities online and then went mainstream on TikTok in 2023 and 2024, often in jokes about getting mogged by a teacher, a cat or a substitute.

How to use it

  • “he got mogged by the substitute teacher.”
  • “stop standing next to me, you’re mogging me.”
  • “the new school uniform mogs the old one.”
