What does “fanum tax” mean?

Taking a bite of someone else’s food without asking. Named after the streamer Fanum.

What it means

If you have food and a friend takes a bite or grabs some fries without asking, you just got fanum taxed. The person doing it is “collecting the fanum tax”. It’s usually playful, like a tax every snack has to pay when friends are around.

Where it comes from

It’s named after Fanum, a streamer and member of the AMP group, who became famous on Kai Cenat’s streams for taking food from his friends. The bit spread in 2023 and became one of the most used Gen Alpha phrases.

How to use it

  • “you got fries? fanum tax.”
  • “my little brother fanum taxed half my somsa.”
  • “lunch was going great until the fanum tax hit.”
