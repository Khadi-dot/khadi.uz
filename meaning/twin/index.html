Twin is what you call your best friend, the person who’s always on the same wavelength as you. It doesn’t matter if you look nothing alike. “Wsg twin” (what’s good, twin) is a common greeting.

Calling a close friend twin took off online in 2024, especially with streamers and on TikTok, and quickly became everyday Gen Alpha slang.
