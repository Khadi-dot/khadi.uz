What it means

Say less means “I understand, you don’t need to explain any more”, usually because you’re already convinced. If a friend offers you food or invites you somewhere fun, “say less” is a quick yes.

Where it comes from

It comes from hip-hop and African American English and has been common on social media since the late 2010s.

How to use it

  • “want plov?” “say less.”
  • “party at my place on Friday.” “say less.”
