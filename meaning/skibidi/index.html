What does “skibidi” mean?

Nonsense word from Skibidi Toilet, a YouTube series about singing heads in toilets fighting men with cameras for heads. Can mean good, bad, or absolutely nothing.

What it means

Skibidi is the ultimate nonsense word. Kids use it for almost anything: something cool, something weird, something bad, or nothing at all. If you hear it in a sentence and can’t work out what it means, that’s because it often doesn’t mean anything. That’s the joke.

It also shows up in phrases like “skibidi toilet”, “skibidi rizz” and “skibidi Ohio”, which mostly exist to sound as chaotic as possible.

Where it comes from

It comes from Skibidi Toilet, an animated YouTube series by Alexey Gerasimov (the DaFuq!?Boom! channel) that started in February 2023. In it, singing heads pop out of toilets and fight people who have cameras, speakers and TVs for heads. The heads sing “skibidi dop dop dop yes yes”, a mashup of “Dom Dom Yes Yes” by Biser King and “Give It to Me” by Timbaland. The series got billions of views and the word escaped into everyday kid language.

How to use it

  • “bro just did a skibidi dance in the middle of class.”
  • “that test was so skibidi. I’m cooked.”
  • “what are you even saying?” “skibidi.”
