What does “rizz” mean?

Charm. The ability to attract someone. Short for charisma.

What it means

Someone with rizz can talk to anyone, especially a crush, and make it work. You can “have rizz”, “rizz someone up” (flirt with them successfully) or have “unspoken rizz”, which means you’re charming without saying a word.

“No rizz” means the opposite: awkward, nervous, zero game.

Where it comes from

Rizz is short for charisma, taken from the middle of the word. Streamer Kai Cenat made it popular, and it spread through TikTok and YouTube in 2022. Oxford University Press named rizz its Word of the Year for 2023.

How to use it

  • “he got her number in five minutes. crazy rizz.”
  • “I tried to rizz her up and forgot my own name.”
  • “unspoken rizz is when you just nod and it works.”
