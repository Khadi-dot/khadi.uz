What does “mewing” mean?

Pressing your tongue to the roof of your mouth for a sharper jawline. The 🤫🧏 gesture means "can’t talk, I’m mewing."

What it means

Mewing means resting your whole tongue flat against the roof of your mouth, which people online claim gives you a sharper jawline over time. Kids do it in photos, in class and in the middle of conversations.

The gesture 🤫🧏 (a finger to the lips, then a tap on the jaw) means “I can’t talk right now, I’m mewing”, and is mostly used to dodge questions as a joke.

Where it comes from

The name comes from British orthodontists John Mew and his son Mike Mew, who promoted tongue posture as part of an approach called orthotropics. It went viral on YouTube and TikTok and became a huge meme around 2023 and 2024. There is little scientific evidence that it changes the shape of your face.
