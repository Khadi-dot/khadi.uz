What does “cooked” mean?

Doomed. In big trouble. No way out.

What it means

Being cooked means you’re in trouble with no way out. Didn’t study for the test? Cooked. Mom saw your report card? Cooked. A person can be cooked, a situation can be cooked, and “we’re so cooked” is the classic group panic.

It can also describe someone who’s exhausted or acting strange: “bro is cooked, he hasn’t slept.”

Where it comes from

It grew out of older slang where “cooked” meant exhausted or ruined, and became everywhere on TikTok in 2023 and 2024, often as “we’re cooked” or “it’s so over”. The opposite is “we’re so back”.

How to use it

  • “exam tomorrow and I didn’t study. I’m cooked.”
  • “the teacher said the test is worth half the grade. we are so cooked.”
  • “my phone is at 2% and I’m lost. cooked.”
