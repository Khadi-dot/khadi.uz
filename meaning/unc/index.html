What does “unc” mean?

An older person who is out of touch. Can be used against anyone over 22.

What it means

Unc is short for uncle, and it’s what kids call someone old and out of touch. You don’t need to be anyone’s uncle: if you still use Facebook, explain memes or say “on fleek”, you’re an unc. Being called unc at 22 is a rite of passage.

Sometimes it’s said with respect, for an older person who still has some cool.

Where it comes from

Calling older men “unc” comes from African American English. In 2024 and 2025 it took off online as a roast for anyone who seems older than their age, including people barely out of their teens.

How to use it

  • “you still use Facebook? okay unc.”
  • “unc doesn’t know what 6 7 means.”
  • “he’s 23 and his back hurts. certified unc.”
