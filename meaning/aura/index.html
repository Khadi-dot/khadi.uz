What does “aura” mean?

Your invisible coolness score. Doing something cringe costs aura. Doing something cold earns it.

What it means

Aura works like a score. Doing something calm, brave or effortlessly cool earns aura. Doing something embarrassing loses it, and people love to put exact numbers on it: “+5,000 aura” for a last-second goal, “−10,000 aura” for tripping on stage.

Someone with a lot of aura has a presence that makes them seem cooler than everyone else in the room.

Where it comes from

The word comes from the old idea of an aura as an energy field around a person. In 2024, TikTok turned it into a points system, with videos ranking everyday moments by how much aura someone gained or lost.

How to use it

  • “he stayed calm when the teacher yelled. infinite aura.”
  • “she waved back at someone who wasn’t waving at her. −1000 aura.”
  • “that walk-out song gave him so much aura.”
