What does “lowkey and highkey” mean?

Lowkey = a little, or secretly. Highkey = a lot, openly.

What it means

Lowkey means a little, secretly or without making a big deal of it. Highkey means a lot, and openly. “I lowkey like that song” means you like it a bit, or don’t want to admit it. “I highkey love that song” means everyone should know.

Where it comes from

Low-key has meant calm or understated in English for well over a hundred years. Hip-hop slang turned it into “secretly” or “kind of” in the 2010s, and highkey was invented as its opposite.

How to use it

  • “lowkey hungry, highkey want somsa.”
  • “I lowkey miss school.”
  • “highkey the best day of my life.”
