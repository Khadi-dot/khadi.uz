What does “brainrot” mean?

Content so dumb it slowly melts your brain. Oxford’s Word of the Year for 2024. You are on a brainrot website right now.

What it means

Brainrot is content so silly, repetitive or nonsensical that it feels like it’s slowly melting your brain: Skibidi Toilet, Italian brainrot, endless short videos and memes stacked on memes. It also describes the state of having watched too much of it, when you can’t stop saying “6 7” or “Ohio”.

It isn’t always an insult. Plenty of people enjoy it on purpose, which is pretty much what this whole website is about.

Where it comes from

The phrase is surprisingly old: Henry David Thoreau used “brain-rot” in his 1854 book Walden. It came back online in the 2020s to describe low-quality internet content, and Oxford University Press made “brain rot” its Word of the Year for 2024.

How to use it

  • “I watched three hours of Skibidi Toilet. full brainrot.”
  • “my little brother only speaks in brainrot now.”
  • “this website is pure brainrot.”
