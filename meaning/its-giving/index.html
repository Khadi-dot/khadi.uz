What does “it’s giving” mean?

It has the vibe of…

What it means

It’s giving means “it has the vibe of” or “it reminds me of”. You say what something is giving: “your outfit is giving final boss”. Said on its own, “it’s giving” means it’s giving something good.

Where it comes from

It comes from Black and Latino LGBTQ ballroom culture, where it described the look or attitude a performer was serving. It spread through TikTok around 2021 and is now used by everyone.

How to use it

  • “your outfit? it’s giving final boss.”
  • “this classroom is giving prison.”
  • “the new haircut is giving.”
