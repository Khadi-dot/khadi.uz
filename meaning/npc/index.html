What does “NPC” mean?

Someone who acts like a background character in a video game. No original thoughts.

What it means

An NPC is someone who seems to have no thoughts of their own, like a background character in a video game who repeats the same lines. If you always say the same thing, follow the crowd or walk around on autopilot, people might call you an NPC.

“NPC behavior” means doing something boring and predictable.

Where it comes from

NPC stands for non-player character, the characters in video games that the computer controls. It became an insult online around 2018 and grew huge on TikTok, where some creators even livestream themselves acting like NPCs.
