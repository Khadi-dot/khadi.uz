Brainrot dictionary

Gen Alpha and Gen Z slang explained for parents, teachers and anyone over 25 who feels lost. Tap a word to see what it means, where it came from and how to use it without sounding like an unc.

skibidi
Nonsense word from Skibidi Toilet, a YouTube series about singing heads in toilets fighting men with cameras for heads. Can mean good, bad, or absolutely nothing.
rizz
Charm. The ability to attract someone. Short for charisma.
aura
Your invisible coolness score. Doing something cringe costs aura. Doing something cold earns it.
aura farming
Doing cool things on purpose just to gain aura. Made famous in 2025 by a kid dancing on the front of a racing boat in Indonesia.
sigma
A lone wolf who is cool without trying. Allegedly at the top of the social ladder.
ohio
Anything weird, cursed or unexplainable. Nothing personal against the actual state.
67 (six seven)
Means nothing at all. Say it with both palms up, moving them up and down. Got so big that Dictionary.com picked it as its Word of the Year for 2025.
fanum tax
Taking a bite of someone else’s food without asking. Named after the streamer Fanum.
mewing
Pressing your tongue to the roof of your mouth for a sharper jawline. The 🤫🧏 gesture means "can’t talk, I’m mewing."
mogging
Looking so much better than someone that they disappear next to you.
cooked
Doomed. In big trouble. No way out.
bussin
Extremely good. Mostly used for food.
no cap
No lie. "Cap" means a lie, and 🧢 means someone is lying.
mid
Average. Mediocre. Nothing special.
delulu
Delusional, usually about a crush or a big dream.
glazing
Praising someone way too much.
crash out
To lose it and do something reckless because you’re angry.
unc
An older person who is out of touch. Can be used against anyone over 22.
chopped
Ugly, messed up, falling apart.
NPC
Someone who acts like a background character in a video game. No original thoughts.
W / L
Win / loss. Can be given to people, ideas or entire countries.
brainrot
Content so dumb it slowly melts your brain. Oxford’s Word of the Year for 2024. You are on a brainrot website right now.
italian brainrot
AI-made creatures with fake Italian names and robotic narrator voices, like Tralalero Tralala and Bombardiro Crocodilo. Took over the internet in 2025.
tung tung tung sahur
A wooden log holding a bat. Comes from Indonesia, inspired by people banging drums to wake everyone for sahur, the pre-dawn meal in Ramadan.
say less
Understood. No more explaining needed.
lowkey / highkey
Lowkey = a little, or secretly. Highkey = a lot, openly.
it’s giving
It has the vibe of…
sheesh
Said when something is impressive. The more e’s, the more impressive.
goated
The greatest. From G.O.A.T., greatest of all time.
twin
Your best friend. The person who gets you.
