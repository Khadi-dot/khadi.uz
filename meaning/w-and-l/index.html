What does “W and L” mean?

Win / loss. Can be given to people, ideas or entire countries.

What it means

W means a win and L means a loss. You can give a W or an L to anything: a person, a decision, a lunch, a whole country. “Take the L” means accept that you lost, and “massive W” means something went great.

Where it comes from

It comes from sports and gaming, where results are written as wins and losses. On social media, people started replying to posts with just “W” or “L” as a quick verdict.

How to use it

  • “free plov at the wedding? W.”
  • “forgot my homework again. huge L.”
  • “W teacher, she cancelled the test.”
