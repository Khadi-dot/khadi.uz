What does “mid” mean?

Average. Mediocre. Nothing special.

What it means

Mid means average, nothing special or a bit disappointing. A mid movie isn’t terrible, it’s just forgettable. It’s one of the quickest ways to shut down something someone else is excited about.

Where it comes from

It’s short for middle, as in middle of the road, and was used in hip-hop slang for years before going mainstream on social media around 2022, especially as a one-word review.

How to use it

  • “the movie was mid.”
  • “everyone hyped that burger and it was mid.”
  • “is it good?” “mid at best.”
