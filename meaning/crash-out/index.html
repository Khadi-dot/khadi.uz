What does “crash out” mean?

To lose it and do something reckless because you’re angry.

What it means

To crash out is to lose your temper and do something reckless, usually over something small. A crash out can be a full meltdown after losing a game or an angry rant in the group chat. Someone who does it a lot is called a crash out.

Where it comes from

It comes from African American English, where crashing out has long meant acting recklessly and ruining your own situation. It went viral in 2024 on TikTok and streams, mostly as a joke about overreacting.

How to use it

  • “he lost one game and crashed out.”
  • “don’t crash out over a group project.”
  • “I was one bad grade away from crashing out.”
